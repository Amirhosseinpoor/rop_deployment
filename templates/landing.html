01 — Ophthalmic AI diagnostics

Reading the eye at the resolution of a subspecialist.

Retinopathy of Prematurity

Infant retinal screening, staged and zoned automatically.

Upload one or many fundus photos per infant. Mediverse segments the vessel tree, disk and macula, then grades disease and localizes it to the optic nerve.

Plus disease classification — Normal · Pre-Plus · Plus via EfficientNet-B4 vessel tortuosity analysis.
Severity & zonal mapping — Stage 0–5 graded and localized to Zone I / II / III.
ICROP 3rd-edition decision support — laser / anti-VEGF vs. 48-hour follow-up recommendations.

Keratoconus & Corneal Topography

Dual-eye ectasia staging with a Siamese network.

The EyeNet architecture compares left (OD) and right (OS) corneal maps pairwise, grading ectasia and scoring structural symmetry between the eyes.

5-tier severity — Normal · ATN · NEIr · EIr · eKCN grading of corneal ectasia.
Bilateral symmetry scoring — SfRS vs. NSfRS refractive-status evaluation across both eyes.
Ocular-linked cardiovascular risk — hypertension & cardiorespiratory estimates from vascular features.

02 — Occupational & whole-patient health

An all-in-one clinical command center per employee.

Demographics, non-invasive anemia screening, extracted lab panels, vitals, a 12-system exam, hazard exposures and work-fitness certification — assembled into one living dossier.

Profile Detail · /health/profile/detail

From smartphone photo to conjunctiva pallor verdict.

Employees upload an eye photo; a two-phase segmentation crop and palpebral mask drive a vision-language pallor verdict — cross-checked against lab hemoglobin.

Lab & panel extraction — CBC, metabolic, lipid, urinalysis and screens parsed to analytes with out-of-range flags.
Spirometry & vitals matrix — FVC, FEV1, ratio and pattern (obstructive / restrictive / mixed) with a 12-system exam.
Work-fitness certification — Fit · Fit with Conditions · Unfit banners with referral tracking.
